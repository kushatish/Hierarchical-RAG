# Fictional Campus Handbook — demonstration data only

Attendance policy. Students must maintain at least 75 percent attendance to sit for final examinations. Medical exemptions require approval from the academic office. Approved exemptions do not change scholarship requirements.

Scholarship eligibility. The merit scholarship requires a CGPA of at least 8.5 and annual family income below 500000 rupees. Students with active disciplinary penalties are not eligible for the merit scholarship. Scholarship applications close on 30 September each academic year.

Placement registration. Final-year students must register through the placement portal before 15 August. A student may hold only one accepted placement offer. After accepting an offer, the student cannot apply for another campus placement unless the placement office grants an exception.

Library policy. Undergraduate students may borrow four books for fourteen days. The late return charge is five rupees per book per day. Reference books cannot be borrowed and must remain in the library.

Hostel policy. Hostel quiet hours run from 22:00 to 06:00. Overnight guests require written permission from the warden. Hostel maintenance requests must be submitted through the facilities portal.

Academic appeals. A grade appeal must be filed within seven days of result publication. The appeal fee is 200 rupees per course. The fee is refunded if the grade changes after review.
